# TASK: Draft the Introduction of a position paper (~700 words)

## Paper
Working title: "Agentic Institutions: Mandates, Capabilities, Constraints, and
Escalation for Machine Economic Actors"

## Thesis (fixed)
AI agents are becoming economic actors with their own money. The unit of
analysis is shifting from "human uses AI tool" to "agent organizations that
hold mandates, capabilities, and constraints, transact with machine money,
and close a sense→reason→act loop." Institutions (contracts, markets, firms)
exist to solve coordination problems; agents need functionally equivalent
institutions: mandates instead of job descriptions, capability registries
instead of resumes, escalation paths instead of managers. Anchors: A2A
protocol, ERC-8004, agent-to-agent payments (x402 etc.).

## The paper's quantitative core (weave these in ;  this is the hook)
The authors operate a live hub-and-worker agent institution (one orchestrating
model + free-tier worker models) and measured its economics:
- Delegated drafting: 22,824 chars of artifact produced by workers at zero
  marginal cost; only 3,853 chars (17%) were admitted back into the hub's
  context (verdict tails + one spot-check slice).
- Cost model (Part III): hub attention persists and is re-paid every remaining
  turn of a session; worker cognition is paid once. Dominance condition for
  delegation: (2−p)[v + (w/h)(S/T̄)] < S where S = artifact size, v = admitted
  chars, p = single-attempt acceptance probability, w/h = worker/hub price
  ratio, T̄ = expected remaining session turns.
- Three consequences: (1) with free workers, delegation dominates for ANY
  acceptance probability (free-lane delegation cannot lose); (2) even paid
  workers may cost ~15× the hub per char and delegation still wins; (3) for a
  fixed context budget, delegation yields ~6× more institutional work per
  session (lifespan extension, not just cost saving).
- Jevons rebound: when cognition is near-free, dispatch volume explodes and
  the binding scarcity migrates from model price to hub verification
  bandwidth. Verification is the new scarcity.
- Structural claim: the four-layer institutional anatomy (mandate =
  the brief, capability registry = worker routing, constraints = acceptance
  needles + budgets, escalation = retry→slice-read→brief-rewrite) is not
  borrowed from human sociology ;  it is the cost-optimal control law for
  delegated cognition. The economics re-derive the anatomy.

2. The gap: protocols standardize communication/identity/payment but nothing

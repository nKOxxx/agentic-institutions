The error survived because the review chain conferred authority: once the chair's required-change list contained the number, it read as a requirement rather than as a claim, and a requirement is not something an author is expected to re-derive.
It points at where to check.
It is not itself the check.
Table 1 is no longer a static claim that happens to match the files; it is a projection of the files that the build re-derives and would refuse to print otherwise.
The gate is therefore strongest exactly where reproduction was already easy, and silent exactly where the record has historically been weakest.
Treat a reviewer-supplied sentence as a pointer to a recount.
The recount is the fact; the sentence is only where the recount was suggested.
Reading the log is therefore not the same as reading evidence.
